One rule. Three states.

Color is collision.

Equal colors make both fighters solid. Attacks connect, combos extend, and knockback can drive a rival into the arena spikes. Different colors break that link: bodies pass through one another and every attack misses.

View controls

Commit

Switch into your rival’s color to open a damage route. Matching also makes you vulnerable.

Convert

Chain short attacks, read recovery, and use heavy knockback to control the lane.

Break link

Change state to phase through pressure, reposition, or deny an incoming strike.
